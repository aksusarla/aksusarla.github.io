How I Think

4 rules

Drop me into something new and messy. In a few weeks I will know how it works, where it breaks, and what to build so the team and its users move faster.

1

Read the rule before the spreadsheet.

Most slow processes are slow because people skip the source. I start from the regulation, the contract or the raw data.

2×

Do it twice, then automate it.

The second time I do something by hand, I start building the tool that does it for the whole team.

0

Secondary sources in the final number.

Every figure I quote traces back to a government order, a filing or a model I can open.

1

Check before anything ships.

I run my own pre-review on structure, numbers and language before work goes to a manager.
